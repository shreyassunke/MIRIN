# Anatomical and figure models

## Male physique — `male-body.glb`

Browser-sized mesh simplified from the ZBrush 2018 sculpt in
`models-src/male_body_files`. That download included no license notice.

Training regions are stored on each mesh as glTF extras (`type`, `name`,
`nameDetail`). Head, hands, feet, and the front of the lower leg stay
visible and are not selectable. The source sculpt was reduced to about
140,000 triangles.

## Female physique — `body-female.glb`

`GEO-body_female_realistic` from Blender Studio
[Human Base Meshes](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/) v1.4.1,
exported at one multiresolution level and Draco-compressed.

- Authors: Blender Studio and community contributors (Dan Ulrich and others)
- License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Bundle: https://download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1.zip

# Gym stations

Each station is welded, decimated to at most 26,000 triangles, turned to its
app-facing yaw, and given one satin steel material on export.

## Seated cable row — `seated-cable-row.glb`

[Seated Cable Indoor Row Machine](https://open3dmodel.com/3d-models/3d-model-seated-cable-indoor-row-machine_193566.html)
(Open3dModel #193566, `.3ds`). License as listed: personal use or education.

## Leg press — `leg-press.glb`

[Gym Leg Press Gym Machine](https://open3dmodel.com/3d-models/3d-model-gym-leg-press-gym-machine_194657.html)
