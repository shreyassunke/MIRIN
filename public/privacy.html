Privacy

MIRIN is a personal training log. This page says what the app stores and how to delete it.

Account

Name and email, from the form you fill in or from Google or Apple if you use those to sign in. Apple can hide your email. MIRIN stores the address Apple gives us.

Profile

Age, gender, and an avatar, if you set them. Age and gender are optional.

Training log

Sets, body weight, protein, and calories. Used only to show you your log. Not sold. Not used for advertising. Not written to HealthKit or iCloud.

Who else sees it

  • Supabase stores the account and the synced log.
  • Google or Apple, only if you choose that sign-in.
  • YouTube, only while a form video plays. The video is not downloaded.

How long it is kept

Until you delete the account. In the app, open Profile, then Account, then Delete account. That removes the login and the synced log. The copy on this device is removed at the same time. Deleting needs a connection.

Children

MIRIN is not for children under 13, and it is not in the Kids Category.

Tracking

MIRIN does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
